Overview

Modernizing an Event Rental Experience

During my practicum at Pinnacle Event Rentals, I worked directly with the client to improve the company's website by designing and implementing customer-focused features that enhanced usability, product discovery, and overall user experience.

The primary goal was to simplify how customers browse rental products, discover event inspiration, and receive immediate assistance while planning weddings, corporate events, and private celebrations.

Throughout the project, I researched AI-powered customer support solutions, evaluated multiple chatbot platforms, developed concepts for an interactive Event Inspiration Gallery, improved product discovery, and created comprehensive documentation that enables future maintenance by the client.

The Challenge

Identifying Business Problems

Pinnacle Event Rentals offers hundreds of rental products across numerous categories including tents, linens, tables, chairs, décor, staging, tableware, and event accessories.

Although the inventory was extensive, customers often experienced difficulty discovering products, understanding complete event setups, and locating answers to frequently asked questions.

The existing website provided valuable information, but navigating between categories required additional effort and often interrupted the customer's planning process.

The challenge was to create solutions that improved customer engagement while remaining practical, scalable, and easy for the client to maintain after project completion.
