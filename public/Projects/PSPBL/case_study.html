Overview

Redesigning a Platform for Global Police Education

The Police Society for Problem-Based Learning (PSPBL) is an international organization dedicated to improving police education through problem-based learning, collaboration, and knowledge sharing.

Our objective was to redesign PSPBL's website so it better represented the organization's global mission, improved accessibility, and delivered a modern digital experience for educators, police officers, researchers, and first responders.

Throughout the project we conducted extensive user research, content planning, information architecture, wireframing, visual design, and strategic planning before preparing the platform for WordPress implementation.

The Challenge

Modernizing an Outdated Digital Experience

The existing website contained valuable educational content, but visitors often struggled to locate important information because of outdated navigation, inconsistent layouts, and fragmented content.

The platform also lacked modern accessibility features, responsive layouts, and an engaging user experience that reflected PSPBL's international reputation.

Our challenge was to create a website that was easier to navigate, visually consistent, accessible across devices, and simple for board members to maintain.
