Overview

Refreshing a Local Brand's Digital Presence

Regent Custom Crest is a well-established embroidery and custom branding company based in Brandon, Manitoba. The business specializes in producing high-quality embroidered apparel, stitched logos, promotional products, and personalized branding solutions for organizations, businesses, sports teams, and individuals.

The goal of this project was to redesign the existing website so it better reflected the company's craftsmanship while creating a cleaner, more professional online experience for visitors.

By improving navigation, visual hierarchy, responsiveness, and accessibility, the redesign provides customers with a clearer understanding of the company's services while strengthening the overall brand identity.

Challenge

Bringing an Established Business into a Modern Digital Experience

While Regent Custom Crest had built an excellent reputation for quality embroidery and custom apparel, the website no longer reflected that same level of professionalism.

Navigation could be improved, the visual design appeared outdated, and important information was not always easy for customers to discover.

The challenge was to modernize the website while maintaining the company's established identity, making it easier for potential customers to understand the services offered and contact the business.

Reflection

Lessons Learned

Working on the Regent Custom Crest redesign reinforced the importance of designing with both business goals and user needs in mind. I learned how thoughtful improvements to navigation, content hierarchy, and responsive layouts can significantly enhance the overall user experience without changing the core identity of a brand. The project also strengthened my front-end development skills and deepened my understanding of accessibility, usability, and responsive design best practices for real-world business websites.
